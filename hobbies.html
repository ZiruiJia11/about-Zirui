Hobbies & Interests

Board Games

I enjoy playing strategic board games in my free time, especially games that require planning, adaptability, and decision-making under uncertainty. These games help me develop logical thinking and problem-solving skills in a fun and social environment.

They also improve how I approach complex problems, as I often need to analyse different strategies, predict outcomes, and adjust my decisions based on new information.

Skiing

Skiing is one of my favourite outdoor activities. It allows me to stay active while enjoying challenging environments that require focus and control.

It has helped me build confidence and maintain composure in fast-paced situations, which I find useful when working under pressure or dealing with complex technical problems.

Hunting

I enjoy spending time outdoors and have an interest in hunting and nature-based activities. These experiences help me develop patience, observation skills, and attention to detail.

Being in a quiet and natural environment also allows me to reset and maintain balance, which helps me stay focused and productive in my work.

Exploring New Technologies

I enjoy exploring new technologies and tools in my spare time, particularly in software development and artificial intelligence.

I often experiment with new frameworks, programming concepts, and development tools to improve my understanding and stay up to date. This habit helps me learn quickly and adapt to new environments.
